# Affordable Generative AI Chatbot for Small and Medium-Sized Restaurants in Australia

A low-cost, responsible AI chatbot concept designed to improve customer service efficiency for SME restaurants through faster responses, reduced staff workload, and 24/7 automated support.

## Problem Statement

Small and medium-sized restaurants in Australia often struggle to manage high volumes of customer enquiries such as reservations, menu questions, FAQs and order-status requests. During peak periods, delayed responses increase staff pressure, reduce customer satisfaction and may result in lost business. Existing AI customer-service tools are often too expensive or too complex for SME adoption.

## Evidence Base

This project is grounded in academic and industry evidence showing that Generative AI chatbots can improve response speed, customer engagement and service efficiency, while also introducing risks related to hallucination, privacy, trust and governance. The project builds on gap analysis using the Jobs-to-be-Done framework and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

## Proposed Solution

The project proposes a lightweight Generative AI chatbot prototype for SME restaurant environments. The chatbot will support:
- reservations
- menu and pricing queries
- frequently asked questions
- order-status enquiries

The design includes human escalation, privacy-by-design controls and responsible AI safeguards.

This project applies responsible AI principles, including transparency, privacy-by-design, human escalation and risk management guided by the NIST AI RMF and Australia's AI Ethics Principles.

## Sustainability

The project supports SME digital transformation through a low-cost and lightweight solution, aiming to improve operational efficiency without requiring enterprise-scale infrastructure.
